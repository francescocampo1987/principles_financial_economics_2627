Deriving the efficient frontier

Markowitz mean–variance model without short sales: minimise portfolio variance for a target return, with weights that are never negative and add up to one. Change the inputs and drag on the chart.

Inputs

Target return

You can also click or drag on the chart to set m.

Risk and return of the selected portfolio

Weights along the frontier

Weights are never negative and always add up to 100%. Between two corner portfolios each weight is a straight line in m, so two portfolios on the same piece generate the rest of it.

Assumptions: short sales are not allowed (every weight is at least 0 and the weights add up to 1), there is no risk-free asset, and returns are summarised by their means and covariance matrix. Adding a risk-free asset turns the frontier into the Capital Market Line (next lecture, CAPM).
