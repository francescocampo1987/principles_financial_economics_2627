Deriving the efficient frontier

The derivation, step by step

    Weights along the frontier

    Weights are never negative and always add up to 100%. Between two corner portfolios each weight is a straight line in m, so two portfolios on the same piece generate the rest of it.

    Assumptions: short sales are not allowed (every weight is at least 0 and the weights add up to 1), there is no risk-free asset, and returns are summarised by their means and covariance matrix. Notation follows the course slides: for the set S of assets held, AS = 1TΣS−11, BS = 1TΣS−1μ, CS = μTΣS−1μ, DS = ASCS − BS2. Adding a risk-free asset turns the frontier into the Capital Market Line (next lecture, CAPM).
