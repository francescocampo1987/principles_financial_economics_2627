The efficient frontier

Markowitz mean–variance model without short sales of the risky assets: minimise portfolio variance for a target return, with weights that add up to one. Change the inputs and drag on the chart.

Inputs

Target return

You can also click or drag on the chart to set m.

Risk and return of the selected portfolio

Weights along the frontier

Weights are never negative and always add up to 100%. Between two corner portfolios each weight is a straight line in m, so two portfolios on the same piece generate the rest of it.

Assumptions: short sales of the risky assets are not allowed (their weights are at least 0 and all the weights add up to 1), and returns are summarised by their means and covariance matrix. There is no risk-free asset unless you give one asset a standard deviation of 0. That asset can then be lent and borrowed at its return (its weight can be negative), and the efficient frontier becomes the straight Capital Market Line through the tangency portfolio T, dashed beyond T where it requires borrowing (next lecture, CAPM).
