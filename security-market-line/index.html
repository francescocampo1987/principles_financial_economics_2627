Point 3: the arbitrage strategy

Assumptions: the CAPM holds for the market portfolio T and the risk-free asset F; you can borrow and lend at RF and sell short without frictions (no borrowing fees, margin requirements or dividends to pay); time-1 amounts are expected values. A strategy exploits a mispriced asset by taking a position in it and the opposite position in a portfolio P of T and F with the same beta: it needs no money today and has no market risk, but earns the mispricing α.
